LaTeX for Outlook

Write math in LaTeX in an email and send it as typeset formulas every recipient can see.

Writing

Open LaTeX pane from the message toolbar and pin it. Type $a^2+b^2=c^2$ for a formula in a sentence, or $$\int_0^1 f\,dx$$ for one on its own line. When you pause, each formula becomes a picture. Back to LaTeX turns the pictures back into text so you can edit them. Anything left is converted when you press Send.

Reading

On a message with LaTeX in it, choose Show LaTeX to see it with its formulas drawn. Click a formula to copy its LaTeX.

Privacy

Everything happens in your Outlook. Message contents are never sent anywhere by this add-in; formulas are drawn on your own device. Settings are kept in your mailbox.
